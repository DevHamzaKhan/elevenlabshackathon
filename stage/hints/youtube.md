# YouTube (com.google.android.youtube)

Opening it: the icon label is "YouTube". It's often in a "Google" folder.

- **Search:** tap "Search" (a magnifier at the top right) → the field "Search YouTube" gets the focus → `type` the search → `key enter`. Results are rows whose description holds the title, channel, views and length, such as "Lo-fi beats to study to - 2 hours - ChillHop - 3M views".
- **Play a video:** tap the result's row by a distinctive word from its title. The video starts in a player at the top.
- **Player controls:** tap the video once to show the controls. They hide again after a few seconds, so read the screen and tap straight away. They are "Pause video" / "Play video", "Next video", and "Enter fullscreen". `key back` shrinks the player into a mini player at the bottom.
- **Bottom tabs:** "Home", "Shorts", "Subscriptions", "You". Stay out of "Shorts" unless asked.

## What goes wrong

- Ads: a "Skip" or "Skip ad" button can show after a few seconds. Tap it when you see it. Don't tap the ad itself ("Visit advertiser", "Learn more").
- The first rows can be ads ("Sponsored") or Shorts. Skip those and pick a normal video row.
- Rotating to fullscreen changes `size`. Read the screen again before tapping by position.
- Don't like, subscribe, comment or share unless the user asked.
