# Gmail (com.google.android.gm)

- **Inbox:** the top bar is a search field labelled "Search in mail". To its left is "Open navigation drawer". To its right is the account picture ("Account and settings"). Bottom right is a big "Compose" button.
- **Email rows:** each row's text or description holds the sender, subject and preview, often all in one ("Sarah Chen, Running late, can we push to 3?, Unread"). The newest email is at the top. To open one, tap it by the sender's name, such as `{"text":"Sarah Chen"}`. Text matching also finds partial matches, so the name alone is enough.
- **Open email:** the sender's name and the subject are near the top. Before replying, check that the sender is the person the user named. At the bottom of the message are "Reply", "Reply all" and "Forward". On a long email you may need to `swipe up` to reach them. Top right are "Archive", "Delete", "Mark unread" and "More options".
- **Reply:** open the email → tap "Reply" → the message body (hint "Compose email") gets the focus → `type` the text with no `on` → tap "Send" (the paper plane at the top right; its description is "Send").
- **New email:** tap "Compose" → in the "To" field, type a name or address, then tap the suggested contact → tap "Subject" and type → tap "Compose email" and type → tap "Send".
- **Search:** tap "Search in mail" → type the search → `key enter` → the results list.

## What goes wrong

- The keyboard can hide "Reply", "Forward" and the bottom of the body. "Send" is always at the top, so it stays reachable. If you need something under the keyboard, `key back` once to hide it.
- An email that's part of a conversation opens the whole thread. "Reply" answers the last message.
- Suggested-reply buttons ("Sounds good!", "Thanks!") sit above "Reply". Don't tap them unless one says exactly what the user wants.
- A tab bar ("Mail", "Meet") may show at the bottom. Stay on "Mail".
- Several accounts: the account picture switches between them. Check you're in the right inbox before searching.
- After sending, a "Sent" message with "Undo" shows for a few seconds. Never tap "Undo".
- `key back` from an unsent email can ask "Discard draft?" or save a draft. Only discard if the user cancelled. Never leave drafts behind.
