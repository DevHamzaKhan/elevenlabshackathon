# Calendar (Samsung Calendar com.samsung.android.calendar, or Google Calendar com.google.android.calendar)

Opening it: the icon label is "Calendar". Check the screen's `app` to know which one opened. The labels below cover both, with Samsung first.

- **What's on today or tomorrow:** both apps open on today. Event rows show as text with the time and title, such as "10:00 AM Team standup". Read them out rather than tapping. For tomorrow, `swipe left` once in day view, or tap the date in month view.
- **Change the view:** Samsung has a menu button ("Open navigation drawer" or "≡") with "Year", "Month", "Week", "Day", "Schedule". Google has the same items in its menu. "Schedule" lists the upcoming events, which is the easiest to read.
- **New event:** tap the "+" button, bottom right (description "Add event" or "Create"). Google may then ask "Event", "Task", "Birthday": tap "Event". Tap the "Title" field and `type`. Then set the start time: tap the time shown under "Starts" (Samsung) or next to the date (Google), and pick from the clock or list. Tap "Save" at the top right (Google) or bottom (Samsung).
- **Open an event:** tap its row. "Edit" (a pencil) and "Delete" (a bin, or under "More options") are at the top.

## What goes wrong

- Time pickers differ a lot between apps and phones. After each tap, read the screen again and look for the time you want as text before tapping "Save".
- An event with no time set becomes an all-day event. Check the "All day" switch is off when the user gave a time.
- Several calendars (personal, work) can show a calendar picker. Leave the default unless the user named one.
- Never delete or edit events unless asked. If you open an event by mistake, `key back`.
